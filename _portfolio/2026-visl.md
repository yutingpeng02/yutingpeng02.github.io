**Mar 2025 – Jun 2026 · ICT, CAS · Beijing, China**

ViSL combines accurate 2D skeletons with 3D-lifted pose augmentation to learn view-invariant representations from frontal-view training data. Cross-view and proxy-assisted cross-stream contrastive losses align viewpoints and pose estimators, while asymmetric constraints mitigate noise in 3D pose estimates during joint training. Only the primary 2D skeleton stream is retained at test time.

**Result:** Achieved state-of-the-art performance on the cross-view benchmark, surpassing prior methods by **9.2 percentage points** with zero additional inference overhead.
