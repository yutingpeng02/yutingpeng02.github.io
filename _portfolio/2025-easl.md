**Dec 2025 – Present · ICT, CAS · Beijing, China**

* Developed EASL, a generative augmentation framework combining 3D pose-guided viewpoint control and pretrained diffusion models to synthesize diverse signing samples while preserving signing structure.
* Proposed sparse keyframe synthesis, which outperformed full-video synthesis under matched generation budgets.
* Introduced keypoint-guided token compression and learnable bottleneck-token fusion to reduce computational cost while preserving signer-centric cues and complementary RGB–keypoint information.
* Built a controlled robustness benchmark comprising **9,575 videos across 67 variation types** to systematically evaluate existing methods and pinpoint failure modes under signer and contextual variations.
