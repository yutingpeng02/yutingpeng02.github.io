**Sep 2024 – Apr 2025 · ICT, CAS · Beijing, China**

XV-SLR reconstructs 3D whole-body meshes from RGB videos, projects skeleton keypoints, and renders depth sequences from arbitrary viewpoints with diverse backgrounds. Its two-stage training pipeline uses single-modal pretraining followed by Transformer encoder fusion, integrating complementary RGB, depth, and skeleton features with auxiliary modality-specific supervision losses.

**Result:** First place in both RGB and RGB-D tracks of the Cross-View Isolated Sign Language Recognition Challenge at WWW 2025.
