**Nov 2023 – Jun 2024 · ICT, CAS · Beijing, China**

Co-operate Sign is an interactive sign language learning system with precise real-time feedback. Its end-to-end pipeline integrates MediaPipe keypoint extraction, multithreaded block streaming to a server, and continuous sign language recognition, typically exceeding 10 FPS during interactive practice.

Temporal alignment and keyword-level replay synchronize user and reference videos for side-by-side comparison. Color-coded feedback on insertion, deletion, and substitution errors guides learning.
